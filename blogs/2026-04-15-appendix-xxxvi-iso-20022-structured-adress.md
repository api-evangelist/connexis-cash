Appendix XXXVI - ISO 20022 Structured adress eliott.coulon Wed, 04/15/2026 - 14:39 View PDF SWIFT accepted a community request to defer the November 2026 deadline for the implementation of structured addresses in ISO20022 payment messages. While structured addresses will remain recommended, a revised timeline will be announced before the end of 2026. Until the new date is confirmed, unstructured address formats for international and treasury transfers exchanged via SWIFT will continue to be accepted.
